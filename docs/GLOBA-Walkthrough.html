GLOBA

Walkthrough & Demo Guide

Your world. Your adventures. Your story.

Made for Dylan & Nicole

How to use this guide

This document does two jobs. Part A is a ten-minute demo script you can follow word for word when showing GLOBA to someone. Part B is a reference to every page, element and feature, with screenshots, so you can look up “what is that thing?” later.

  • Screenshots show sample data: seven saved places, two lit up, one visited, one 3-month itinerary. Your own app will start empty.
  • Photos come live from Wikipedia, so they may differ slightly from what you see here. Places without a photo show clearly-labelled generated artwork.
  • Every piece of information is labelled Live (fetched just now from a named source), Curated (our editorial sample data, not verified), Estimate, Demo or Your entry. Nothing sample is ever passed off as live.

Before you demo

  • Open the app (see the deployment steps in the pull request) and tap Enter your world, or skip with Esc.
  • Save 3–5 places first (Cape Town, Lisbon and Queenstown work well) so the Saved, Our World and map screens are not empty.
  • On a phone, add it to the home screen for the full-screen app feel.
  • Sound is off by default. Turn it on in Settings → Sound if you want the light-switch and ambience.
Part A · The ten-minute demo

The story to tell

GLOBA is a shared adventure platform: discover places you might live for weeks or months, save them, plan them, and light them up on your shared globe. Keep that arc in mind: find → save → plan → light up.

Part B · Page-by-page reference

On a computer there is a slim left rail; on a phone a bottom tab bar. The five main places are the same on both.

2 · Explore: the globe

  • Drag to rotate, scroll / pinch to zoom, hover or tap a country to highlight it and open its panel.
  • Search bar (top): type a place, a country, or a sentence. It suggests places, countries and live worldwide towns as you type.
  • Filter button beside the search opens the filters drawer.
  • Globe / Map toggle (top right) switches view.
  • + / − / home (right) zoom and reset the view.
  • Layers (bottom left) shows or hides marker types.
  • The little spaceship with Dylan and Nicole drifts across; tap it for a quip.

3 · Explore: search, preview, map

  • Save adds to the shared collection; tap again to remove (with Undo).
  • Add to itinerary lets you pick a trip and day, or create a new trip.
  • Light it up takes you into the miniature world for that place.
  • Open full brief goes to the full guide (section 5).
  • Extraordinary nearby lists striking landmarks within reach; tap one to fly there.
Switching Globe ↔ Map keeps the selected place. The globe pans left when a panel is open so the place isn't hidden behind it.

4 · Discover

  • Mode badge (top right): “Demo mode” = built-in keyword understanding; “AI mode” appears when an AI key is configured.
  • Reasons list: green ticks = what matched; amber = something to check.
  • Budgets: estimates are in US dollars. Other currencies are compared only if live exchange rates are available, and it says so when they aren't.
  • Nothing fits? It says so plainly rather than inventing a result, and offers a worldwide town lookup.

5 · The destination brief

Every place opens as a full guide. Sections are labelled with where their information comes from.

6 · Saved places

  • Considering / Planned / Visited are separate. “Lit up” is a separate filter, since lighting a place never means you visited it.
  • Search notes and tags; sort; group by country or destination.
  • Add notes and tags on any card; both of you edit the same collection.
  • Remove shows an Undo so nothing is lost by accident.
  • Cards list which itineraries a place is in.

7 · Trips (itineraries)

The guided builder

Eight short steps: purpose → where → when → how long → focus → budget → pace → which saved places to include. Optional steps can be skipped.

  • Regenerate a day for a fresh offline suggestion.
  • Overlapping times are flagged.
  • Changes save as you type (“Saved locally”). Duplicate and Delete (with Undo) are at the top.
  • Meals are generic (“Lunch — local café”). Real venues come from Suggest More.

8 · Light It Up: the miniature world

Saving a place can end with a small celebration. From any place choose Light it up.

  1. Arrive. The camera swoops down onto a floating island. The glowing beam shows where to go.
  2. Walk. W A S D or arrow keys, drag to look around (phone: joystick). Your partner follows beside you and waves if you pause.
  3. Activate. Get close, then press E / tap the big gold button.
  4. Celebrate. The sky turns golden, flowers bloom, lanterns light. Dylan does his victory dance; Nicole claps then jumps.
  5. Return. A card confirms it, the camera pulls back, and the real location lights up on the globe.
  • Skip lights the place immediately; ✕ / Esc leaves without lighting.
  • Lighting is recorded once and never changes Saved → Visited.
  • If 3D isn't available the app offers “Light it up anyway” (a simple 2D version) so nothing is blocked.
  • Sound (optional): light-switch, success chime, gentle ambience; off until enabled in Settings.

Thirteen worlds, chosen for each place

The world matches the place: seven continent styles plus desert, rainforest, lake, alpine, coast and volcano. Camels and pyramids in the desert, giraffes and elephants on the savanna, pagodas and pandas in Asia, penguins and an aurora in Antarctica, and more.

Preview any world by adding ?theme=desert (or savanna, asia, europe, northamerica, southamerica, oceania, antarctica, rainforest, lake, alpine, coast, volcano) to a /light/<place> address.

9 · Our World

  • No streaks, no daily pressure, nothing invented.
  • “Countries explored” counts only places marked Visited.
  • Recent lights list who lit what and when.

10 · Settings

  • Travel preferences per person (interests, climate, stay length, budget, remote-work needs) feed “Picked for you” in Discover.
  • Appearance: light, dark or follow device; reduced motion.
  • Sound: off by default; volume slider.
  • Units & currency: kilometres or miles; home currency.
  • Your data: Export a backup, import one, or erase everything. Data lives in this browser only, so back it up and use it to move between devices.

11 · The travel assistant

  • Try: “Find five places like this one”, “Which of our saved places is best for three months?”, “Compare Lisbon and Chiang Mai for remote work”, “Build a relaxed three-day itinerary”.
  • It refuses to name restaurants from memory and points you to Suggest More instead.
  • Every answer is badged: Demo answer or AI-generated from retrieved data.

12 · On your phone

The phone version isn't a squeezed desktop: there is a bottom tab bar, bottom sheets instead of side panels, a thumb-sized joystick and big action buttons, and it works in landscape too.

Add to Home Screen: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen; in Chrome use the menu → Install app. It then opens full screen with its own icon.

14 · Troubleshooting & FAQ

ProblemWhat to do
My saved places aren't on my other phoneData is per browser. Settings → Export backup on one, Import backup on the other.
Photos show “Illustration”No photo was found (or the photo service was unreachable). It's generated artwork, and it's labelled.
Suggest More says it's unavailableThe free OpenStreetMap service is busy or blocked. Try again in a minute; search links are offered meanwhile.
Map is dark / no streetsTiles couldn't load. Country outlines and your markers still work. Configure MAP_TILE_URL for a dedicated provider.
The globe or miniature world won't loadYour device may not support 3D graphics. A simplified globe and a 2D “Light it up anyway” are used, and everything else works.
Things feel slow on an older phoneTurn on Settings → Reduced motion; close other tabs.
“Demo mode” everywhereNo AI key is set on the server. Everything still works.
I erased everything by mistakeImport your latest backup, so export regularly.
